# Fixoku legal values required before publication

These values are intentionally not guessed. Replace each marker during legal
review and before production publication:

- `LEGAL_VALUE_REQUIRED`: exact seller legal entity and trade-register details
- `LEGAL_VALUE_REQUIRED`: tax number / tax office, MERSIS or equivalent identifier
- `LEGAL_VALUE_REQUIRED`: final registered address and authorized representative
- `LEGAL_VALUE_REQUIRED`: customer-service phone and operational email addresses
- `LEGAL_VALUE_REQUIRED`: payment, shipping, return and complaint operating details
- `LEGAL_VALUE_REQUIRED`: retention periods and approved cross-border transfer safeguards
- `LEGAL_VALUE_REQUIRED`: IYS operator/account and approved commercial-communication process

These documents are drafts for review and are not attorney approval.

## Official review sources

Legal review should use the current texts and guidance published by the
[KVKK Authority](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/), [Mevzuat Bilgi Sistemi](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/),
[Ticaret Bakanlığı](https://www.ticaret.gov.tr/) and the official
[İYS information site](https://www.iys.org.tr/). No random legal-template site
is treated as authority.
